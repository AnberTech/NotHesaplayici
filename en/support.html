Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your courses, grades and preferences are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Can I change the grade scale?

Yes. In Settings you can edit letter-grade ranges, grade points and pass conditions to match your university.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings → Language and pick one of 17 languages. By default the app follows your device language.

Is the PDF transcript official?

No. The PDF is for information only and has no official validity.

I changed phones — where is my data?

Data lives on your device, so it depends on your device backup (iCloud / Google backup). Restore from your backup.

Is the app paid? Are there ads?

No. The app is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
