How is GPA calculated?

The grade points of each course are multiplied by its credits (or ECTS). These products are added up and divided by the total credits: GPA = Σ (grade points × credits) ÷ Σ credits. Your term GPA is the same calculation using only that term's courses.

Worked example

Calculus is 4 credits with an A− (3.7), Physics 3 credits with a B (3.0) and Biology 3 credits with an A (4.0). The weighted total is 4 × 3.7 + 3 × 3.0 + 3 × 4.0 = 14.8 + 9 + 12 = 35.8. Total credits are 10, so your GPA is 35.8 ÷ 10 = 3.58.

Term GPA and cumulative GPA

Your term GPA covers the courses of one term; your cumulative GPA covers every course you have taken. The app groups your courses by term and keeps track of both.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my GPA?

Multiply the grade points of each course (A = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3 …) by its credits, add the results and divide by the total credits.

Should I use credits or ECTS?

Whichever your school uses to weight averages. The formula is the same; only the weighting number changes.

Do failed courses count towards my GPA?

Usually yes, with 0 grade points. When you retake and pass a course, many schools replace the old grade; the rule depends on your school.

Can I convert my GPA to a percentage?

Conversion tables differ by country and school and are usually not linear. This calculator gives your GPA on a 4.0 scale.

Can I save my courses?

The web calculator does not save anything. In the Grade Calculator app you can save your courses term by term and keep your GPA up to date.
